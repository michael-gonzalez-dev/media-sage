The Courage Post

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

The Courage Post ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by Onos Monos. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled during the app's beta testing period.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address, a password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see it in plain text), and an optional display name.

Content you create. Bookmarked headlines, memorized quotes, pinned figures, your weekly figure schedule, and reflection notes you write. Reflection notes are encrypted before they leave your device and can only be decrypted by your account.

Usage analytics. We use Firebase Analytics to record app usage events — for example, when you sign up, sign in, open or bookmark a headline, memorize a quote, pin a figure, or change an appearance setting. These events are tied to an anonymous device/install identifier, not directly to your name.

Crash reports. We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive diagnostic information (device model, OS version, stack trace) if the app crashes, so we can fix bugs.

What we don't collect. We don't run ads, use advertising SDKs, or sell your data to anyone.

Beta Testing Notice

This app is in active development and currently distributed to external testers via TestFlight or Google Play. Features may change, and — because this is a beta — your data may occasionally be reset as we test new functionality. We'll make a reasonable effort to warn you before any reset that affects saved content.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing support@thecouragepost.app. We'll process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app develops. Material changes will be noted in the app or communicated by email.
