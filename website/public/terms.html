About the Content

The figures

The historical and biblical figures in this app, including their portraits, biographies, quotes, and daily reflections, are here for education and devotional reflection. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by any of these figures, their estates, or any institution connected to them.

What's made with AI

  • Quotes. They come from a curated library, and each one is tied to its source. AI matches them to each day's headlines.
  • Daily reflections. Written by AI in a figure's voice, based on their verified writings and meant to stay true to what they taught. They are not the figure's own words, and I don't claim that the figure wrote or said them.
  • Portraits. AI-generated illustrations. Each one is based on historical research and checked by hand, but they are artistic interpretations, not actual likenesses.

Why use AI at all

A lot of people have real concerns about AI, including its energy and environmental costs, what it means for workers and jobs, the power it puts in a few hands, and how it affects the way we learn and think. Those concerns are fair. The Courage Post is not an endorsement of AI. It's my attempt to use it in a narrow, careful way, as a tool that points you back to what these men and women taught and the faith they held, never as a substitute for it. Building it is also how I'm learning, first-hand, what this technology can and can't actually do, rather than relying on hype or speculation.
