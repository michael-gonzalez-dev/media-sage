The Courage Post

Coming soon

The Courage Post is an app built around a daily briefing grounded in the wisdom of those who lived the Christian faith and those who witnessed its impact on the world. Each briefing looks through a lens you choose: the day's headlines, the writings of the day's reporter, or a theme such as hope, grief, or justice. Separately, the day's headlines are paired with sourced quotes from their writings. The app is in beta testing.
