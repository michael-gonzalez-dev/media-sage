The Courage Post is an app built around a daily briefing grounded in the wisdom of those who lived the Christian faith and those who witnessed its impact on the world. Each briefing looks through a lens you choose: the day's headlines, the writings of the day's reporter, or a theme such as hope, grief, or justice. Separately, the day's headlines are paired with sourced quotes from their writings. The app is in beta testing.

Why The Courage Post

A lot of the news today is designed to keep us anxious, because anxious readers keep scrolling. The Courage Post is built to do the opposite.

Each day's briefing grounds you in the hard-won wisdom of people who lived through real hardship long before us and found in God a confidence and peace worth passing on. The day's headlines are paired with that same wisdom.

My hope is that a few minutes here each day leaves you steadier than the news alone would.
